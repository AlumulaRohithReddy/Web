It Ends With Us

It Ends with Us by Colleen Hoover follows florist Lily Bloom as she builds a new life in Boston, finds romance with charming neurosurgeon Ryle Kincaid,
but her abusive past echoes when her first love, Atlas Corrigan, reappears, forcing her to confront her traumatic childhood and make the difficult choice to break the cycle of domestic violence for her future and her unborn child. The story explores themes of love, abuse,
and strength as Lily navigates her intense relationship with Ryle and her lingering connection to Atlas, ultimately choosing to end the abuse rather than repeat her parents' cycle

Man Searching For Meaning

Man's Search for Meaning by Viktor Frankl is a non-fiction book detailing his harrowing experiences in Nazi concentration camps, presenting his psychotherapeutic method, logotherapy, which posits that finding purpose is life's primary motivation, even in suffering, through tasks, love, or courageously facing hardship.
Frankl argues that humans possess the ultimate freedom to choose their attitude in any situation, a key to survival, and emphasizes that meaning isn't found in happiness directly, but through self-transcendence by serving a cause or loving another person.

Robin Cook

Robin Cook's novels are fast-paced medical thrillers, often featuring recurring characters like forensic pathologist Jack Stapleton and his wife Laurie Montgomery,
that delve into the dark, unethical, and often dangerous sides of the healthcare industry, exploring timely issues like bioweapons, stem cell research, medical tourism, and insurance fraud, all while blending thrilling mysteries with societal commentary and exposing potential dangers lurking within modern medicine

One Day Life Will Change

One Day Life Will Change, by Saranya Umakanthan, is a heartwarming fiction novel about Samaira, a talented singer who loses her parents, love, and passion,
becoming dejected until she meets Vivian, a successful entrepreneur who inspires her to regain hope, rediscover her dreams, and fall in love, making it a story of resilience, inspiration, and second chances.
The book follows their budding romance, focusing on mutual support, overcoming heartbreak, and finding purpose again, making it a popular choice for new fiction readers
